Mod made for ModCon V over the weekend. Create an estate for your ruler of feudal lands and grow your keep to support your activities of leisure. This mod has 3d models that will show on map and 2d art when you grow your estate. Feel free to kick back and relax in medieval luxury.

[h1]Lord over your Estate.[/h1]
[list]
[*]Unique buildings for you manor and surrounding fields
[*]Edits to vanilla buildings while under feudalism
[*]Court position to lead while you are away
[*]Events and decisions based on buildings you hold
[*]Custom art as you grow your keep.
[*]Special test of how relaxed you are
[/list]

[IMG]https://i.imgur.com/F0Ex7IK.png[/img]

[h2]Requirements and Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Requires the [b]Roads to Power[/b] expansion (estates use the domicile system).
[*]Works with existing saves, though a fresh start is recommended.
[*]Overrides the domicile window GUI and the tournament activity. Mods touching those files may conflict.
[/list]
